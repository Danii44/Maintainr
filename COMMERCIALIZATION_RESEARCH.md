# Commercialization research notes

## Public onboarding patterns observed

| Platform | Observed public motion | Relevant lesson for Maintainr |
|---|---|---|
| Buildium | The public navigation exposes both **Schedule a Demo** and **Free Trial**, with a trial email form and demo-video alternative. | Offer a guided route for larger or more complex portfolios alongside a self-service route for smaller or ready-to-start organizations. |
| TenantCloud | The public Home presents **Get Started**, **Sign Up**, and a 14-day free-trial message while explaining benefits for multiple roles. | Use a simple trial/workspace entry point, but make the role-specific value and portfolio fit clear before registration. |
| RunFleet | Its public demo begins with a short workspace intake rather than entry to a shared customer account. | A prospective organization should get a separate workspace or a disposable tenant-specific demo, never a shared production workspace. |
